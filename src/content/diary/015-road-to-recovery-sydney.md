**4x4 Ownership**

****

Team Jollyfollies are about to begin their 4x4 driver training with a course given by [Land Rover Owner Adventure Club](http://www.lroac.com/), which is the bargain price of £65 for two people for a day. Obviously at this price the training will be limited group training, but as a starter it cannot be snooted at. We will attend this on the 4th November and will give a full write up here. If it is good we will be joining these bad guys for an adventure weekend out in Wales, which again is nice and cheap, and will allow us to test out our new found skills in a controlled environment. And the added bonus of this is it's on Ollie's birthday, so a pressie's in the making for Jenny. Hint Hint.

As well as this we are going to sign up for a mechanics course that starts in January. Again this is a bargain, at only £99 per person for an 11 week course. You can also bring your own vehicle, and hence get a good servicing in for the price! :) We will be taking this course at [Eastleigh College](http://www.eastleigh.ac.uk/courseDetails.asp?bookcode=MVMAN1&course=pt)which is on both our ways home from work.
