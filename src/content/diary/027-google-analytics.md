**Google**

We have subscribed to a Google product, [Google Analytics](http://www.google.com/analytics/) which keeps track of the visitors to your website, and also their behavior when on you website. On top of this you can see which sites have referred you, and what words people were searching on when a search engine sent them to you. Now if that doesn't excite you you must be lying in a coffin, six feet under.

From this we can see we are averaging about 10 unique visitors a day, much more that we expected. This has inspired Ollie to try and update the website more frequently, as well as maybe, finally, a bit of spell checking. Another surprising statistic is that we have had visitors from 19 different countries. The Internet is really a global tool.

**Maps**

****

Ollie has spent the last few weeks playing with maps, and mapping software and this has meant that the Jollyfollies crew are now quite far down the road of creating their mapping solution for the trip. The new [PDA](http://www.jollyfollies.com/Reviews/Kit.html)has proven a great success, including the software that was included with it, I-GO. This has mapping all the way to and including Russia, which is a nice bonus. Beyond that we are going to use [Oziexplorer](http://www.oziexplorer.com/) with maps that we scan in ourselves. A few practice runs at this has proved successful.
