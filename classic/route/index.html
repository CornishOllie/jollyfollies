The Route

The plan was simple to say and hard to do: point east from Land's End and keep going until the road ran out at the far side of Australia. Across Europe to Poland and Hungary, down through Romania to Turkey, across Iran to the Caspian, over the roof of Central Asia through the 'stans, down the Karakoram Highway into Pakistan and on into India.

The legs, west to east

Britain · Germany · Poland · Hungary · Romania · Turkey · Iran · Azerbaijan · Uzbekistan · Tajikistan · Kyrgyzstan · China · Pakistan · India · and onward to Australia.
