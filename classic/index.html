The Trip

Jenny and Ollie drove from Land's End, Cornwall to Sydney, Australia in a 1990s Land Rover Defender called DINO, raising money for VSO. This is the diary they kept along the way, written at the time and kept exactly as it was.

The adventure left Land's End on 13th April 2009 and reached Australia the following year. Follow the whole thing in the blog, trace the route, or meet DINO.
