Saturday 25th April 2009 - Fame at Last!

Ollie's Dream

As regular followers of this blog will know Ollie is a proud Cornishman, who likes nothing more that a bit of world wide fame, and hence it will come as no surprise to you that he was delighted for the start of the trip to be published in the Cornishman, a paper that every self respecting Cornishman devours from cover to cover. Jenny was also pleased to be included, although she does not quite understand the implications of being part of a 'St Just couple'. One day she will understand.

(Click photo to see readable larger print)

This though was only our first piece of fame, not only did we appear in the Cornishman, but we were also photographed (papped) as we were leaving Cornwall, and Mark was good enough to send us the photo, which you can see below. This really is the start of world domination.
