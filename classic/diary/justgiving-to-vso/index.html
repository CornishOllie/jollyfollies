Monday 23rd October 2006 - JustGiving to VSO

As frequent guests to these pages will know, as part of our trip we want to raise as much money as possible for VSO. To help people give straight to VSO easily we have set up a JustGiving page. Justgiving is the leading charity fundraising website, enabling ordinary people to raise extraordinary amounts of money for the causes they care about.

Since their launch in 2001, their member charities have collected over £100 million in online donations through their website. We hope to add to this!

Our target is £30,000, which we know is a very large sum. But we believe that personally we can raise £10,000 and hope to raise the rest through corporate sponsorship, but that can only happen a little nearer the time. We aim to do at least one major fundraising event each year leading up to our departure in 2009, starting with the Ollie running the London Marathon in 2007.

If you do wish to donate now, and help our total from looking so small and lonely you can, by following the link below, or going to our website and following the links there. Please remember is you are a UK tax payer to tick the gift aid box and give another 28p in the pound. Many thanks from us and VSO if you do donate :)
