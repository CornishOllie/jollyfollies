Wednesday 31st May 2006 - Exciting Day

Good News Comes in Twos

Today was a day back at work for me, after having 5 days off to play golf at La Manga in Spain. Quite a shock to the system, but had a couple of good pieces of news to keep me going.

Nr 1

It would seem that we have found ourselves a charity that is happy to be associated with our trip. This may sound easy, and in fact wasn't hard, but it was something we felt we had to consider carefully.

Nr 2

On top of this we have been in contact with a couple who are thinking of selling their Landy. Although we are not necessarily in the market to buy our vehicle yet, we feel if the right one came along at the right price then we could buy. It's certainly something to get the juices going, whatever.
