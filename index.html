A travel diary, restored

Two people, one old Land Rover, and a very long way to drive.

In April 2009 we pointed DINO east from Land's End and didn't stop until we reached Sydney. This site is the diary we kept along the way, written at the time. It raised money for VSO, and the site was built by hand in Dreamweaver. It has been rescued from the web archive and rebuilt, with the words as we wrote them, typos aside.
